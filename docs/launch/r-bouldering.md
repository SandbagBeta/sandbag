**Title:** I'm building a free open-source way to walk around a boulder in 3D before you go (looking for testers and feedback)

I'm a student building SandBag. The idea: someone scans a boulder with their phone, uploads it, and the rest of us can orbit it in 3D, see where the start holds, line and topout are, and vote on the grade. Think "beta you can walk around", before you hike in.

<SCREENSHOT/GIF of the wall scan with a line drawn>

**What it is today (no spin):**
- You can upload a phone scan, it's checked automatically, a reviewer approves it, and then you can mark holds and a line on it.
- It's free, open source, and the data is CC BY-SA, so it can't get locked up later.
- It's early. There is no hosted site yet <or: it's live at <DEMO URL>> and very few boulders. I don't want you to hike anywhere based on this.

**On access:** if an area is closed or sensitive, the owner can mark it hidden and we never publish its coordinates. I'd rather have fewer boulders than burn an access point.

**Where I could use you:**
- If you've scanned a boulder, would you try uploading it? Tell me where it was confusing.
- Would you actually use this? What's missing?
- Does the line-drawing match how you'd describe a problem?
