# Data license

All community-contributed content on SandBag — 3D scans, hold annotations, climb descriptions, paths, and grade votes — is licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)**.

Full legal text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode

What this means:

- **You can** share, adapt, and build on the data — including commercially.
- **You must** credit the contributors (per-boulder exports include an attribution list) and license derivatives under CC BY-SA 4.0.
- **Contributors agree** to this license when uploading, and must only upload scans they captured themselves (or have rights to).

Climbing area/route metadata displayed via the [OpenBeta](https://openbeta.io) API remains under OpenBeta's licensing and is credited to them.

The source code of SandBag itself is separately licensed under [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE).
